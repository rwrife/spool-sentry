# Spool Sentry v0.1 risk register

Ratings are planning priorities, not measured probabilities. `H/M/L` mean high/medium/low relative likelihood or impact for this hobby product. Residual risk is accepted only within the observation-only boundary and must remain visible in user documentation.

| ID | Hazard/failure | Likelihood | Impact | Controls and required response | Planned evidence | Residual boundary |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Unsuitable USB source, short, ESD, or overcurrent damages low-voltage electronics | M | M | SELV USB only; datasheet-backed protection; current margin; labeled test points; current-limited bring-up stop conditions | Schematic analysis, ERC/DRC, bench current/rail measurements | Not a power/safety controller; suitable enclosed supply required |
| R-02 | Load-cell overload or off-center force bends mechanics, binds platform, or contacts PCB | M | M | ≥5 kg rated cell for 2.5 kg range; isolated force path; hard mechanical clearances; centered-load guidance | CAD review, static inspection, progressive centered/off-center bench loads | No people, hazardous loads, universal fit, or durability claim |

## Stop conditions

Stop power/bench work on unexpected current, rail voltage, heating, odor, visible damage, intermittent USB, mechanical binding, load-cell yielding, or any reading that lacks an explicit quality state. Disconnect USB before mechanical adjustment. These are future bring-up requirements, not evidence that bring-up has occurred.
